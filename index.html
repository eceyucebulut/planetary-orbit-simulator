Equations used

Newton's law of universal gravitation F = G m M / r²
Planet's acceleration (equation of motion) a = −(G M / r³) r
Numerical integration (velocity-Verlet) xn+1 = xn + vn Δt + ½ an Δt²
vn+1 = vn + ½ (an + an+1) Δt
Kepler's third law (period) T = 2π √(a³ / G M)
Vis-viva equation (live check) v² = G M (2/r − 1/a)
Initial conditions at perihelion rp = a(1−e),  vp = √[ G M (1+e) / a(1−e) ]
Kepler's second law (constant areal rate) dA/dt = L/2 = ½ √[G M a (1−e²)] = πab / T
Orbit shape r(ν) = p / (1 + e cos ν), p = a(1−e²)

Constants: G = 6.67430×10⁻¹¹ m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻², M⊙ = 1.98892×10³⁰ kg, 1 AU = 1.495978707×10¹¹ m. The planet is a test particle on a 1 AU semi-major axis orbit; its mass (1 M⊕) is used only for the force readout.
